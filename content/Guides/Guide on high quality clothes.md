#guide
## Fabrics
Natural fibers are usually higher quality
Synthetic (avoid):
- Polyester
- Nylon
Natural:
- Cotton
- Linen
- Wool
- Silk
- Hemp

Look at fiber content. Its not wrong to buy synthetic fibers, but don't overpay.

## Stitching
Look for good quality specially near the collar area. High quality stitching is consistent with spacing and moves straight across the seam line.

## Features
Non stretch ribbon around the collar will keep the integrity of the collar for longer.
Make sure hardware is well secured
